FrameWise — Privacy Policy

*Effective date: 2026-09-07* FrameWise ("the app") is a photo gallery that organizes your photos on your device. This policy explains what happens to your data. ## Summary FrameWise collects no data. FrameWise shares no data. Nothing you own or that the app produces ever leaves your device. ## Details **All analysis is on-device.** FrameWise uses on-device machine learning (image labeling, face detection, text recognition, barcode detection) to sort photos into categories. These models run entirely on your device. No photo, thumbnail, detected text, tag, or any other analysis result is ever uploaded or sent anywhere. **No network access.** The app does not hold the Internet permission. The operating system itself blocks any network access by the app. **Read-only photo access.** FrameWise requests access to your photos and videos (with your permission) to build its index. Your original photos and videos are never modified, moved, or deleted by the app. **No accounts, no tracking, no advertising.** There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising. **Backups.** FrameWise opts its searchable index (the on-device database of detected text and tags) out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. If you move to a new device, FrameWise rebuilds its index there from your photos. Your analysis settings (charging-only, Wi-Fi-only) may be carried over through Android's standard backup of app settings. **Permissions.** The app requests media-read permissions (photos and video library access) to perform its function, plus standard permissions required by its background-analysis scheduler (see the Play Store listing for the current list). ## Changes If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here. ## Contact For questions about this policy, contact: hello@digvijayrawat.me
