FrameWise — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-02

FrameWise ("the app") is a photo gallery that organizes your photos on your device. This policy explains what happens to your data.

Summary

FrameWise collects no data and performs no uploads. Analysis stays on your device. You can explicitly share a selected photo, video, or exported document using Android's Sharesheet; the receiving app then handles that content under its own privacy policy.

Details

All analysis is on-device. FrameWise uses on-device machine learning (image labeling, face detection, text recognition, barcode detection) to sort photos into categories. These models run entirely on your device. No photo, thumbnail, detected text, tag, or any other analysis result is ever uploaded or sent anywhere.

No network access. The app does not hold the Internet permission. The operating system itself blocks any network access by the app.

Photo access and your actions. FrameWise requests access to your photos and videos (with your permission) to build its index. Analysis and document view adjustments never modify originals. Optional duplicate cleanup deletes only the items you select and approve in Android's confirmation dialog. Share and Play video grant the receiving app temporary read access to the selected original. PDF export creates a local, temporary file and opens the Sharesheet only after you request an export. No media is shared automatically.

No accounts, no tracking, no advertising. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising.

Backups. FrameWise opts its searchable index (the on-device database of detected text and tags) out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. If you move to a new device, FrameWise rebuilds its index there from your photos. Your analysis settings (charging-only, Wi-Fi-only) may be carried over through Android's standard backup of app settings.

Permissions. The app requests media-read permissions (photos and video library access) to perform its function, plus standard permissions required by its background-analysis scheduler (see the Play Store listing for the current list).
